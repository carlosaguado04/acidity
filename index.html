Acidity

Acidity started in 2026 because I got sick of paying for apps that feel half-done. If I’m going to live in this stuff all day, I want it to actually work. So I started building the things I use.

That’s the rule. I don’t put something out that I wouldn’t keep on my own machine. No ads. No tracking. I don’t want your data.

I also build websites when someone has a product and needs the web part done properly — product sites, marketing sites, a real backend when a pretty shell isn’t enough. Same filter. If it isn’t sharp, it stays here.

One person. Small studio. That’s the whole thing.
