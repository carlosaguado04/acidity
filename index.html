Acidity

· · ·

Acidity started in 2026 because too much software feels unfinished. If you spend your day inside an app, it should work — reliably, quietly, without asking for more than it needs.

So I began building the tools I actually use.

That is still the standard. Nothing ships that I would not keep on my own machine. No ads. No tracking. Your data stays yours.

Acidity is a one-person studio with two separate lines of work.

The first is software. These are my apps — products I design, build, and maintain for my own use, then release. They are not built for clients. They are not commissioned, white-labeled, or customized to someone else’s brief. They exist because I needed them to work a certain way, and that is the version that goes out.

The second is web work. Product sites, marketing sites, and backends for people who already have something to put online and need it done properly — not as a pretty shell, but as a site that can carry the product.

What that looks like in practice:

I ship when the work is finished, not when a version number looks good.

The apps do not carry ads, trackers, or quiet data collection.

Privacy is a default, not a feature page.

The studio stays small on purpose. One person means fewer layers between the problem and the thing that solves it.

If you need a site built with care, that is the work I take on.

One person. Small studio. That is the whole thing.
