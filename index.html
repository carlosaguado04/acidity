Mac apps that don’t nag, don’t upsell, and don’t get in your way. They just sit there and work.

Studio

Independent. Opinionated. Useful.

Acidity is an indie MacOS studio that builds apps focused on real user experience, usability, and design that actually does something. No monthly fees. No ads. No tracking. We started this because we were sick of software constantly nagging you to upgrade, shove features you never asked for, or just feel like it was made by people who don’t use their own shit. We couldn’t find the tools we actually wanted, so we built them ourselves—simple, useful, and ours. The software is opinionated. We trust you’ll still find it useful.

Founded in 2026 out of the optimistic belief that people will actually use good software and pay for it when it doesn’t treat them like a subscription farm.
